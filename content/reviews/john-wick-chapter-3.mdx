## The Setup: An Hour to Live, A City to Survive

*Chapter 2* ended with John Wick being declared excommunicado. He broke the rules by killing Santino D'Antonio on Continental grounds. He has one hour before every assassin in the world is coming for him. He has a $14 million bounty on his head. He has no friends. He has no safe place.

*Chapter 3 – Parabellum* picks up exactly where the last film ended. John is running through the rain, bleeding, desperate. The first hour is a relentless chase. He fights a giant in a library. He fights a group of assassins in a knife shop. He fights his way through a horse stable and escapes on horseback. It's the most intense opening hour in the franchise.

---

## The Plot: The High Table Strikes Back

The Adjudicator (Asia Kate Dillon) arrives at the Continental to punish Winston (Ian McShane) and the Bowery King (Laurence Fishburne) for helping John. Winston is given seven days to resign. The Bowery King is stabbed and left for dead. The High Table is asserting its power, and no one is safe.

John seeks help from Sofia (Halle Berry), an old friend in Casablanca who owes him a blood debt. Sofia is the manager of the Casablanca Continental, and she has two dogs — Belgian Malinois she trained to be lethal. The sequence where the dogs attack a group of assassins is both hilarious and genuinely brutal. It's the kind of action that only *John Wick* can deliver.

---

## The Action: Knives, Dogs, and Motorcycles

The knife fight in the antique weapons museum is the film's standout. John and a group of assassins throw knives at each other in a room full of glass cases. The choreography is stunning. The creativity is off the charts. It's the kind of sequence that makes you wonder how anyone thought of it.

The motorcycle chase is also excellent. John fights off assassins on motorcycles while riding a horse. It sounds absurd. It is absurd. It's also incredible.

---

## The Acting: Keanu and Halle

Keanu Reeves is still perfect. He's playing John Wick as a man who is running on fumes, driven by pure survival instinct. He's not enjoying this. He's not a hero. He's a man who wants to live.

Halle Berry is a fantastic addition. Sofia is not a love interest. She's not a sidekick. She's a peer. She's as dangerous as John, and she has her own code. Her chemistry with Keanu is natural and unforced.

---

## The Verdict: A Worthy Chapter

*Chapter 3* is not as tight as the first film. The middle act slows down, and the ending is abrupt. But the action is better than ever, the world is richer, and the performances are excellent. It's a worthy addition to the franchise.

**Score: 8.5/10**

**Verdict:** Must Watch. The dogs. The knives. The horses. It's pure action cinema. Just accept the absurdity and enjoy the ride.
