## A Fairy Tale Written in Shadows

*Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown-Ups* is not designed as a conventional action entertainer. Its title suggests a world where fairy-tale imagery meets crime, ambition, violence, and adult moral conflict. The result is a stylised film that places atmosphere and visual identity at the centre of its storytelling.

## Plot, Ambition & Tone

The story operates in a heightened world where power is rarely clean and ambition often comes with a price. Instead of presenting its characters as simple heroes and villains, the film explores relationships shaped by control, fear, loyalty, and survival.

The fairy-tale influence is darker than traditional fantasy. Its imagery is not soft or comforting; it often feels twisted, theatrical, and unsettling. This gives the film a recognisable identity, even when the narrative becomes difficult to decode.

## Yash's Screen Presence

Yash is the film's strongest asset. His performance relies on stillness, physical confidence, and a controlled sense of danger. He does not need to dominate every scene through dialogue because his body language often communicates the character's authority.

The role also requires emotional ambiguity. The protagonist is not simply a powerful man moving from one fight to another; he is shaped by personal conflict and complicated relationships. Yash handles the larger-than-life moments effectively, while the quieter scenes provide glimpses of vulnerability.

## Direction, Action & Visual Design

Geetu Mohandas gives the film a distinct visual language. Dramatic lighting, elaborate production design, stylised compositions, and heightened locations create a world that feels removed from ordinary reality.

The action is designed for impact. It combines the scale of commercial Indian cinema with the darker mood of a crime drama. The best sequences are those in which the physical conflict reveals something about the characters.

However, the film sometimes prioritises style over clarity. Certain developments may feel cryptic, and some supporting characters appear to need more space. The pacing is also uneven, with moments of strong tension followed by stretches that feel overextended.

## The CinePulse Verdict

*Toxic* is an ambitious experiment in combining star-driven action with a mature fairy-tale aesthetic. It may not appeal to viewers who prefer straightforward storytelling, but its commitment to mood and visual identity makes it distinctive.

**Final verdict:** A bold and visually striking action drama that works best when its dark atmosphere, character conflict, and spectacle remain in balance.
